## Reward policy and wallet

Existing source matrix retained: one point = 25 paise; Essential 1x, Advanced 1.5x, Premium/prestige 2x; service base points general checkup 1000, grooming 800, vaccination 750, ear cleaning 200, haircut 200. Free general checkup is 500; other free services zero in the new standard policy. `clinic-2026-09-13` is the policy version. Rates were delegated by the user; profitability is not claimed without cost data.

New redemptions use manager-approved, immutable whole-bill threshold policies in careSettings/redemption and careRedemptionPolicies. Each tier consumes its exact point threshold for a percentage of the ENTIRE staff-recorded bill, including all line items and taxes. The server snapshots bill total, percentage, points and policy version. No default rates are activated or represented as approved. Draft policies cannot redeem. The former 25-paise conversion, eligible-subtotal percentage and monthly/invoice caps are legacy transaction behavior only; new reserveTier does not impose those caps. The old reserve API requires an explicit legacyCreditEnabled flag and is disabled by default. Historical reservations remain completable/reversible on their recorded terms. Existing listed membership benefits remain independent, subject to clinic clarification; no promises are silently erased.
Subscriptions are recorded by a manager with actual payment reference and paid period in server-only careSubscriptions; profile currentPlan never grants a multiplier. A confirmed appointment snapshots eligible pending points and multiplier. Cancellation, rescheduling or a missed visit cannot debit earned balances. Verified completion atomically writes its global evidence claim, care ledger, wallet earn and audit. Prior nonstandard point/credit ratios remain reconciliation-required rather than silently revalued.

Wallet ownership is per parent; pet checkup entitlements remain separate. Existing users.pawPoints is displayed separately and never mutated. A manager must match its exact current value before a one-time legacy import. Reservations require a staff-recorded real invoice, sufficient spendable points, invoice uniqueness and the active threshold policy. Expired reservations are released transactionally on wallet reads. They expire after 15 minutes and cannot be applied across a month boundary. Parents can release them; staff records actual invoice discount application. Retry IDs, global billing evidence and atomic transactions prevent duplicate spend. Invoice discount reversals restore points once. A verified service refund reverses only its original earn; if already spent, the wallet is held for reconciliation, preserving unrelated history. Managers can settle that specific refund once related billing adjustments restore sufficient available points.

No external cash/payment processor is connected. Clinic staff must actually apply the discount in billing before confirming it here. No new membership is marked paid from a frontend click. Staff controls cover invoice registration, verified subscription, reconciliation, application and reversal. Existing balance migration and real staff operations have not been performed.
